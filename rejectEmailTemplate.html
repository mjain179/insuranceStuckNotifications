HOW TO HANDLE INSURANCE REJECTION

This is Motus Nova with an update regarding the insurance claim for your Motus Hand. Your insurance claim for the Motus Hand was denied. We are not sure of the exact reason yet, but we will be investigating the reason and trying to gather information on whether an appeal can be filed to overturn the decision.

As a patient referred by a partnering clinic, you are still eligible to receive the Motus Hand at no cost for 60 days due to your therapist's referral. Here are your options moving forward in the process:

IF YOU ALREADY HAVE THE MOTUS HAND:

OPTION 1: Continue to pursue Insurance Coverage and appeal their decision

You may receive a denial letter in your mail from your insurance company. In the case that you would like to appeal the decision, please use the instructions on the detailed letter to start the appeal process. Please let us know your intent to appeal the decision.

If insurance denies the appeal, you may continue using the Motus Hand for the remainder of the 60 days, starting from the original delivery date. If the 60-day window has passed, kindly use the prepaid shipping label to return the Motus Hand in its original packaging.

OPTION 2: Accept the insurance denial decision and continue use of the Motus Hand for the remainder of 60 days before returning the Motus Hand or discussing payment plan

*If you have the Motus Hand already, you are welcome to continue using the Motus Hand for the full 60-day no-cost period, counted from the date the Motus Hand was delivered.

IF YOU DO NOT ALREADY HAVE THE MOTUS HAND:

OPTION 1: Obtain the Motus Hand and appeal the insurance claim denial (once you receive and sign for the Motus Hand you will then follow the above steps to proceed)

OPTION 2: Proceed with 60-day no cost option. You are still eligible to receive the Motus Hand at no cost for 60 days due to your therapist's referral.

Understand if you accept the 60-day no cost option below, Motus Nova and our billing partners will no longer pursue insurance reimbursement for the Motus Hand.

After the 60 day trial, you may speak with one of our Financial Aid Team members regarding payment options to continue use of the Motus Hand OR you may return the Motus Hand using the prepaid shipping label and original packaging at no cost.
